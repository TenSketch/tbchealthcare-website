**PrisMax Acute Care System** by **Baxter** is an advanced, next-generation device engineered for continuous renal replacement therapy (CRRT) and multiple organ support therapies in critical care settings. Built on over 20 years of expertise, PrisMax helps clinicians deliver safe, accurate, and efficient treatment in the ICU while reducing workload and improving patient outcomes:contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}.

### Uses

- **Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy (CRRT):** Provides precise, continuous renal therapy for critically ill patients with acute kidney injury.
- **Multi-Organ Support:** Designed for a broad range of extracorporeal therapies, supporting complex ICU treatment needs.
- **Critical Care Units:** Ideal for intensive care environments requiring high accuracy, speed, and system performance.

### Benefits

- **Maximised Efficiency:** Automates labour-intensive tasks and reduces treatment interventions, improving workflow and saving nursing time:contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}.  
- **Improved Accuracy:** Delivers 99.8% of prescribed patient fluid removal with intelligent pump adjustments to maintain treatment precision:contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}.  
- **Simplified Setup:** Features an intuitive touchscreen interface and therapy profiles that streamline setup and reduce human error.  
- **Enhanced Safety:** Integrated safety checks, colour-coded indicators, and liquid level sensors ensure reliable therapy delivery.  
- **Comprehensive Training:** Offers on-device and online interactive training modules for clinicians to learn safely and efficiently.  
- **TherMax Blood Warmer Integration:** Provides precise control of return blood temperature between 35°C and 38°C for optimal patient comfort:contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}.
